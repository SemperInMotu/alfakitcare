Resilience

Reducing legacy risk

A system that is 10–20 years old fails in predictable ways. Below are the jobs almost nobody buys until an outage forces them to.

Restore drill

We restore your backup into a test environment and measure how long it takes. “Backups are running” and “restore works” are different claims, and only the second one is provable.

Test environment

Many installations simply do not have one: changes go straight into production. We separate the test and production tracks so releases stop being a lottery.

Customisation inventory

We reverse-document what previous developers left behind: what each agent does, where its data comes from, and what breaks if you switch it off.

Versions and licensing

The upgrade path for IBM Domino and HCL Notes, the licensing model, what stops working on the way and in which order to fix it.

Enabling your own admin

We hand routine work to your team: logs, rights, agent restarts, standard diagnostics. Our goal is that you call us less often, not more.

Cases

Anonymised. Format: problem → action → result.

  1. Payment import from the accounting system

    Problem: duplicates and truncated invoices when loading multiple currencies.
    Action: CSV exchange, import configurations, tests against real bank statements.
    Result: customer payment import confirmed on production accounts.

  2. E-invoicing documents

    Problem: the XML failed the operator’s validation.
    Action: templates plus checks on company details and regions.
    Result: documents accepted by the e-invoicing operator.

  3. Reporting for CFO, sales and CEO

    Problem: the only cuts available were heavy TMS views.
    Action: daily exports into QlikView, response time in hours, role-based scenarios.
    Result: separate dashboards for finance, sales and management.

  4. Dispatcher and manager roles

    Problem: staff could see other people’s deals and processes were blocked.
    Action: role model, ACL, targeted rights restoration.
    Result: access and processes match the org chart again.

  5. Quotation process optimisation

    Problem: requests were lost between departments and response time was never measured.
    Action: as-is and to-be design, queue roles, a response standard, export into BI.
    Result: a transparent queue and handling time measured in hours.

  6. TMS export into management reporting

    Problem: manual Excel assembly across several parts of the installation, mismatched keys.
    Action: stable export agents out of Domino, agreed fields for finance and sales.
    Result: daily cuts without manual stitching. A full warehouse from any source runs at Semper In Motu.
